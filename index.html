Comparison Guide

Yields vs Adobe's Brand Visibility

Both tools help brands become visible in AI research moments. Both solve real problems, but they solve different problems in unique ways that compliment one another. Use this guide to understand which tool fits your client's immediate need—and when you need both working together.

At a Glance

Two Tools. Different starting points.

Merkle's Yields audits, enriches, and optimizes. Adobe's Brand Visibility measures and benchmarks. Most enterprise clients eventually need both, but they solve different problems first.

Merkle's Yields

The Service-Orientated Approach

  • Assess your commerce catalog or content. Identify gaps. Guide enrichment opportunities using Merkle's proprietary technology across multiple LLMs and Google Commerce, specifically.
  • Yields' UCP methodology analyzes ecommerce sites for ecommerce agentic readiness, AI measurement, and prompt support. Provides optimizations and workflows.
  • White-glove managed service with customer validation at key gates.
  • Merkle handles execution or provides clients with the recipe and files to do it themselves.
Adobe's Brand Visibility

The Pure Platform Approach

  • SaaS platform you operate (or an agency operates for you).
  • Not specific for ecommerce.
  • See your visibility across 8 LLMs. Compare with competitors. Track attribution via real-time dashboards.
  • Provides ongoing measurement for AI visibility across multiple LLMs, agentic tracking/actions on the website, and referral analytics.
  • You own the optimization decisions.
Neither replaces the other. Yields solves "what do we fix?" Adobe's Brand Visibility solves "how visible are we?" Most clients that sell products might license both solutions.

How They're Delivered

Service vs. Platform: What That Means for Clients

Yields is delivered as a managed service, existing client value add service, or complimentary pre-sales activity, all of which Merkle handles execution. Adobe's Brand Visibility is a self-serve platform you operate directly, but can only use it through a paid SaaS model directly with Adobe. Here's how they differ across five key dimensions.

Yields

Managed Service (White-Glove)
Merkle's Role
Facilitate light discovery, setup brand, core catalogue/product to assess in platform, orchestate the automated assessment and execution recommendations, measurement setup, ongoing optimization.
Adobe's Role
Refer clients where there is white space, especially those operating substantial commerce capabilities or challenges or right before a large EDS/Commerce migration.
Timeline
Diagnostic (1-3 weeks) → Execution (3+ weeks) → Ongoing optimizations from there.
Cost Model
No cost to assess. Cost to implement corrections (per engagement type).
Access
Internal Merkle tool only at this time. TBD on client direct access.

Adobe's Brand Visibility

SaaS Platform (Self-Serve)
Adobe's Role
License, demo operate dashboards, maintains infrastructure, adds features, coordinate with Merkle or partner agency for execution.
Merkle's Role
Support executional needs and guidance with Adobe's Brand Visibility, especially leading up to major platform shifts or migrations.
Timeline
Setup (1-4 weeks) → Real-time measurement ongoing. Quarterly reviews recommended.
Cost Model
SaaS licensing (per-brand or multi-brand tiers). Free Chrome plugin for basic assessment.
Access
Direct client or agency dashboard access. Multiple user seats with role-based permissions.
The Compound Approach

How We Can Work Together to Amplify the Right Level of Investments in These Solutions

Many of our joint clients are already Adobe's Brand Visibility customers or exploring it. Yields is a natural strategic add-on that makes their Adobe's Brand Visibility data richer and their ROI more defensible, especially for those in the commerce business.

Yields answers "what do we fix and why?" Adobe's Brand Visibility answers "how much does it matter and when are we winning?" Together they eliminate guesswork from web content and commerce strategy. Here's the typical sequence:

Month 1

Diagnosis + Baseline

Yields reveal what's missing with detailed reports that are both execution and executive ready to review. Adobe's Brand Visibility shows the cost of missing it, further enriched by SEMrush insights in SEO. Client approves enrichment strategy with both data points in hand.

Months 2-6

Execution + Real-time Tracking

As Yields provides enrichment opportunities with ease of integration for application of those enhancements, Adobe's Brand Visibility dashboards show before/after visibility impact. This becomes your ROI proof for broader expansion.

Months 6+

Ongoing Optimization

Adobe's Brand Visibility + Yields continue to flag new gaps and enhancements with competitive intelligence. Yields prioritize which gaps to close next quarter. Compounding returns become quantified and defensible.

How Optimization Happens: Depending on whether you're using Merkle managed services to apply the changes or doing it on your own, these tools provide guidance on what to optimize across Site/CMS, Content, Google Commerce, and more.
